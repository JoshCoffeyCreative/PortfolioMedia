Project Summary

Designing a learning experience

I designed an interactive educational game with the aim to make learning chemistry more engaging to young audiences. The process focused on guiding users through structured activities with clear instructions, feedback and clues.

Implications

Making Education intresting

I designed the experience In JavaScript implementing drag and drop, click detection and multiple-choice score-based sections. Visual and audio feedback was used to engage and guide the users through the experience reinforcing the content they were learning.

Development

How the project evolved.

This project was divided into the stages of research, prototyping,refinement, final developement

Supporting Documentation

Stages of the process.

Industry research

A sample of educational games were observed and documented. User profiles were used to understand the influence the examples had.

Prototyping interaction

Paper prototypes of several concepts tested the flow of each activity. Task procedure such as drag and drop was trialled

Feedback and refinement

Paper prototypes revealed flaws in interaction logic. The most effective prototype was developed to have more clarity and clearer interaction.

Final designs

Interaction logic was built within JavaScript and animation was built in CSS.
